Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Target: Google Play Compliance
This Privacy Policy applies to the DocuKit: PDF & Image Tools mobile application (Package: com.aibackgroundstudio.app). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes how DocuKit Developer Team ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). If you have any questions regarding this policy, you may contact us at: support@docukitapp.com.

1. Information Handled by the App

Device-Only Processing (Photos, Documents & Scans)

The App requests access to selected photos, files, and camera strictly when you initiate an operation (such as document scanning, PDF conversion, image compression, photo resizing, or background editing). Core operations run directly on your device. We do not upload or store your private documents or source photographs on our servers during standard local workflows.

Camera & Storage Permissions

The App requires camera permission strictly for document scanning and photo capture. Storage/Media access is requested solely to load images you select and save the processed files back to your device.

Optional Online Services & Public Content

When you choose to use the public video search or browse job listings, your search text and basic request headers are processed to deliver relevant results. Search queries may be logged in aggregate for performance analytics and security abuse prevention.

Microphone & Voice Search (Optional)

If you activate the optional voice search feature, microphone permission is requested. Voice audio is processed locally or via Google's device speech service into search text. We do not record or retain your raw audio recordings.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

DocuKit displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its ad partners may collect and process certain device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, general network location, and app diagnostics to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements in compliance with applicable laws.

To learn more about how Google handles information in partner apps, visit: Google Privacy & Terms for Partner Apps.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

All created PDFs, edited images, and temporary cache files remain on your device and are subject to your control. You may permanently delete them at any time from within the App or by clearing the App's data in Android Settings.

You can also request the deletion of any optional analytics records associated with your installation by emailing our support desk at support@docukitapp.com.

4. Security

All external network communications (such as fetching public job listings, video meta, or ad delivery) are strictly transmitted over secure HTTPS (TLS encryption) protocols to prevent unauthorized interception.

5. Children's Privacy

DocuKit is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

You have full control over your privacy:

  • You can revoke Camera, Photos, or Microphone permissions at any time in Android App Settings.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • You can clear all app cache, temporary projects, and search history directly within the App's settings screen.
